# Support

Highlights for ChatGPT is designed to work without an account or cloud
service. Before opening a support request, reload the extension, refresh the
ChatGPT conversation, and confirm that the latest release is installed.

When reporting a problem, include the extension version, browser name and
version, operating-system version, and steps that reproduce the issue. Do not
include private ChatGPT conversation text, exported highlight backups,
credentials, or other sensitive information.

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, open an issue at:

https://github.com/O0000-code/highlights-for-chatgpt/issues

Issues are public. Do not include private conversation content, exported
highlight backups, credentials, email addresses, or other personal data. This
project has no support database or access to the highlights stored in your
browser.
